### Boards your agents fill in

Ask Nova for a board and it builds one — typed fields, ordered stages, and cards its agents fill. Drag them in the dashboard, and arm a stage so the work runs the moment a card lands there. Connector-backed boards optional; the old /kanban and /tickets pages moved in.
